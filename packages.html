Before you book

Do I need to buy parts first?

Parts and fluids are extra. You can buy them, or I can bill at cost. For a first oil lesson, having the filter and oil on hand keeps the session moving.

If you are teaching my teen do I need to stay?

Really its your call, I am here to teach someone who is capable. You are more than welcome to get your hands dirty or just watch and ask questions.

Whose tools?

Yours or mine. If you have a jack and basic sockets, great. If not, I bring what the job needs.

Where do you teach?

Kuna and surrounding areas, on your car. Born and raised here — Willing to travel to surrounding areas for a small travel fee. Prefer my shop? Bring the car and lets rock! (Not a drop-off service.)
