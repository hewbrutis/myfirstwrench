What we cover

Oil

The default visit

$85, about 90 minutes. Oil, filter, and a tire rotation. Find the filter and drain, put it back together without wrecking the pan, check the level, then rotate the tires. Next oil change, they already know.

Spare

Spare tire and jack

Add $40 if we do it the same day as the oil lesson. If that is the only skill they need, it is $65 — message me. Jack, lug nuts, spare, put the car down without guessing. Floor to come out is $65.

Brakes

The long skill

Brake pads on one axle (front or rear — two wheels), about 2 hours / $130 if booked on their own. That is a longer day than oil, so it is not priced as another $85 visit. Before you book

Do I need to buy parts first?

Parts and fluids are extra. You can buy them, or I can bill at cost. For a first oil lesson, having the filter and oil on hand keeps the session moving.

Does a parent have to stay?

Parent can watch. For younger teens, stay. For older teens, your call. I am there to teach, not to babysit a car in the street.

Whose tools?

Theirs or mine. If you have a jack and basic sockets, great. If not, I bring what the job needs.

Where do you teach?

Kuna and surrounding areas, on your car. Born and raised here — I am not driving in from Boise for this. Prefer my shop? Bring the car and lets rock! (Not a drop-off service.)
