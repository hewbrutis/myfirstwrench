What we cover

Oil

One of three skills

Oil and filter is a One Skill Session ($125) if that is the job you pick. Find the filter and drain, put it back together without wrecking the pan, check the level. Next oil change, they already know.

Tires

Spare tire / tire change

Tire work is a One Skill Session ($125) if that is the job you pick. Jack, lug nuts, spare tire, put the car down without guessing. Core 3 and Full Basics already include tires.

Spare tire

On your car, in the driveway

Same One Skill Session if spare tire and jack is the skill you pick. I bring a jack if you do not have one. Core 3 and Full Basics already include this.

Brakes

The long skill

Brake pads on one axle (front or rear — two wheels) can be the skill you pick for a One Skill Session ($125). Core 3 bundles oil, tires, and those pads. Full Basics adds rotors plus two extras like headlights / taillights, spark plugs / coil packs, fluids checks, or air / cabin filters.

Before you book

Do I need to buy parts first?

Parts and fluids are extra. You can buy them, or I can bill at cost. For a first oil lesson, having the filter and oil on hand keeps the session moving.

Does a parent have to stay?

Parent can watch. For younger teens, stay. For older teens, your call. I am there to teach, not to babysit a car in the street.

Whose tools?

Theirs or mine. If you have a jack and basic sockets, great. If not, I bring what the job needs.

Where do you teach?

Kuna and surrounding areas, on your car. Born and raised here — I am not driving in from Boise for this. Prefer my shop? Bring the car and lets rock! (Not a drop-off service.)
