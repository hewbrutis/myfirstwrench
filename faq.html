Before you book

Where do you teach?

Kuna and surrounding areas, on your car. Born and raised here — I am am totally willing to travel a bit outside the area for an extra gas fee. Prefer my garage? Bring your car and lets rock! (Not a drop-off service.)

Who is this for?

Anyone and everyone who wants to learn how to work on their own car! While I focus heavily on teaching teens, I coach adults too. Teens can have their parents watch, and adults can bring their kids along! I teach everyday people how to handle oil changes, tire rotations, spare tires, brakes, rotors, and minor suspension work so they can stop paying expensive mechanic bills.

What does a lesson cost?

Oil Lesson is $85 for about 90 minutes. Core 3 is $230. Full Basics is $360. Parts and fluids are extra — you buy them, or I bill at cost.

Is tire rotation included?

No. Oil and filter is the $85 visit. Tire rotation is +$40 the same day, or $65 if that is the only skill. Spare tire and jack is priced the same way. If by itself then +$40 the same day, or $64 if that is the only skill. If you bundle both a Rotation and Spare tire change you save $20.

Do I need to buy parts first?

No, but if you have them, we can jump right to work!! If not, I can order/purchase what is needed and you simply reimburse me what I paied.

Does a parent have to stay?

No, parents don't have to stay—but your teen does need to be ready and willing to learn! I am here to coach and pass on real skills, so this should be something they genuinely want to do. I want to make sure your teen gets their hands dirty and actually learns, rather than watching me do the work. That said, parents are always welcome to hang out, watch, or even jump in and help. My goal is to teach, guide, and be a positive role model for your kids.

Whose tools?

Theirs or mine. If you have a jack and basic sockets, great. If not, I bring what the job needs.

Do you take payment on this website?

Payments taken after the job is finished. Message me, we pick a time, and you pay when the work is done. No card on this site.

Do I have to sign a waiver?

Yes. Working on a car has real risk — jacks, fluids, moving parts. You cannot hold Raz or My First Wrench liable if someone gets hurt. If the student is a minor, a parent or guardian has to agree. The booking form will not send until you check that box.

Can you teach dirt bikes or four-wheelers?

I can teach almost anything to do with engines, gas and diesel, small and big, 2-stroke and 4-stroke. Just message me with what you are thinking and we can go from there. But to specifically answer 'dirt bikes or four-wheelers', Yes! we can do basic maintenance on dirt bikes and four-wheelers. Things like - oil, chains, tires, filters. If I can teach it on your machine, we will figure out how to make it happen. I am just a message away.
