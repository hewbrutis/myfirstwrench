How a visit goes

No shop. No lecture. Your car. I show up in the driveway. I teach you how to safely crawl under your own car. I will not do the job for you. I make sure you can do it the next time without me.

Have a check engine light staring you down? We can plug in, pull the diagnostic codes, and figure out exactly what your car is trying to tell you. Once we know the issue, you can decide if you want to handle the fix yourself with me coaching you through it.

Got a new aftermarket upgrade but feeling stuck on the installation? If you need a little guidance to get it wired up or bolted on correctly, send me a message and we’ll take a look at it together.

Parents are always welcome to watch, or even jump in and get their hands dirty too. Whether you are a beginner learning the ropes or an adult wanting to master your own vehicle, this space is open to anyone ready to learn.
