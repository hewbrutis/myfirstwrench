About

I’m that guy.

The neighbor you call when you or someone in your family has a flat tire, a squeaking brake pad, or a dashboard light staring you down. We master the skills together, in your driveway, so you walk away with total peace of mind.

My Mission

Basic car maintenance is becoming a lost art, and so many of us never get the chance to feel the pride and financial freedom of working on our own wheels. I was lucky—my grandfather taught me nearly everything I know under the hood. He always used to say, "You shouldn't pay anyone for something you can do yourself."

When he passed away a few years ago, I knew I wanted to keep his passion alive. This business is my way of keeping that valuable knowledge in the community: one driveway, one car, and one confident driver at a time, no matter your age or skill level.

As a Marine Corps Veteran born and raised right here in Kuna, I’m proud to serve our local area. I bring these hands-on lessons directly to driveways in Kuna and our wonderful surrounding neighborhoods to help drivers of all backgrounds build skills, independence, and confidence under the hood.

Why people ask me to come help

What they actually want is a supportive guide to walk them through the job step-by-step. Whether you are a teen getting your first vehicle, a busy single parent who wants to be self-reliant, or an adult tired of facing massive mechanic markups, you are completely welcome here. I like doing this, I'm patient with it, and I'd much rather help you learn the ropes safely on your own car than have you get stuck stranded on the side of the road.

My First Wrench is built entirely around your hands-on education. We do driveway lessons tailored to your vehicle—and I am always happy to look at dirt bikes, four-wheelers, or custom project jobs that aren't listed on the main packages page. Just send me a message and we'll take a look.
