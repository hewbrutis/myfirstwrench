About

I’m that guy.

The neighbor you call when your teen has a flat tire and nobody at home knows where the jack goes. We teach them the skills; you get the peace of mind.

My Mission

Basic car maintenance is becoming a lost art, and so many great teens never get the chance to feel the pride of working on their own wheels. I was lucky—my grandfather taught me nearly everything I know under the hood. He always used to say, "You shouldn't pay anyone for something you can do yourself."

When he passed away a few years ago, I knew I wanted to keep his passion alive. This business is my way of paying it forward to the next generation: one driveway, one car, and one proud student at a time.

As a Disabled Marine Corps Veteran born and raised right here in Kuna, I’m proud to serve our local community. I bring these hands-on lessons directly to driveways in Kuna and our wonderful surrounding neighborhoods to help teens build skills and confidence that last a lifetime.

Why people ask me to come help

What they actually want is someone to teach their teen. Not every teen has a parent who knows this stuff. A lot of single parents wish someone would just show them. I am good at it, I like doing it, and I would rather they learn it on their own car than wait until they are stuck on the side of the road.

My First Wrench is that teaching. Driveway lessons on their car — and I will look at dirt bikes, four-wheelers, and jobs that are not on the packages page if you message me.
