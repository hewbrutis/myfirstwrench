About Raz

Marine Corps Veteran teaching real-world car repair in your own driveway.

My Mission

My grandfather taught me how to work on cars. He always said, “If you can’t explain it simply, you don’t understand it well enough.”

That’s why I do this. I want to give people the knowledge and confidence to maintain their own vehicles instead of being at the mercy of shops.

Why people ask me to come help

Most people don’t want to pay dealership prices or feel taken advantage of. They want someone honest who will show them what’s actually going on with their car.

I bring the tools, the knowledge, and the patience. You turn the wrenches. By the end, you understand your vehicle better and save a lot of money.
