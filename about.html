About

I’m that guy.

The neighbor in Kuna you’d call if your kid had a flat and nobody at home knew the jack.

My Mission

Basic car maintenance is becoming a lost art, and so many teens never get the chance to learn how to work on their own vehicles. I had a grandfather who taught me nearly everything I know under the hood. He always said, "Never pay anyone for something you can do yourself."

He died a few years ago. This is how I pay it forward: driveway, their car, next generation.

I’m a Disabled Marine Corps Veteran. I was born and raised here in Kuna. I teach in Kuna driveways, and nearby if the drive makes sense.

Why people ask me to come help

What they actually want is someone to teach their kid. Not every kid has a parent who knows this stuff. A lot of single parents wish someone would just show them. I am good at it, I like doing it, and I would rather they learn it on their own car than wait until they are stuck on the side of the road.

My First Wrench is that teaching. Driveway lessons on their car — and I will look at dirt bikes, four-wheelers, and jobs that are not on the packages page if you message me.
