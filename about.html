About Raz

Marine Corps Veteran born and raised right here in Kuna, Idaho.

My Mission

My grandfather always said, “If you’re gonna do something, do it right.” That’s how I was raised. That’s why I started My First Wrench — to teach people real mechanical skills without the intimidation or gatekeeping.

Why people ask me to come help

On your car. In your driveway.

You do the work. I hold the flashlight.

I’m not a discount mechanic—I’m the patient neighbor with the flashlight. My goal is to make sure you walk away completely confident in your own abilities. Anyone, at any age, can master these skills. Whether it’s oil changes, swapping tires, or fixing brakes, we’ll handle it together on your car, right here in your own driveway across Kuna and the surrounding areas.
