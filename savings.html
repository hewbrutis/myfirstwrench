The Math

Pay the shop every time, or learn it once.

Shop labor comes back. The lesson fee does not. Oil, pads, rotors — what it costs them to do it vs what a shop bills.

Shop invoice vs parts in the driveway

Pick a job. Left is what a shop typically bills. Right is what the parts cost when they do the work. The lesson fee is separate, and you only pay it to learn the job.
